A SUMMARY OF BLACK:

🖤 BLACK — STORY SUMMARY Black was one of the biggest threats in the Duckey story. It started when a duck, 1000 years ago, was almost dead—at about 1 quark/electron of health. The duck found a strange knight-like suit. When Duckey encountered the duck 1000 years later, half of the duck and suit were black, with broken clocks inside the black half. Black tentacles emerged from the black half. The duck itself was clueless about them. The tentacles attacked Duckey, and Duckey fought them with his sword. Cutting them didn't permanently stop them—they kept coming back. Eventually, the black half spread across the duck's entire body. Eyes appeared in the black, and it began laughing. Black became powerful enough to threaten /kill Duckey and /kill Mallard. Duckey was eventually /kill'd and ended up in Banlands, then later Terminatedlands. Mallard was infected by Black. Black began spreading its infection across the world. Quack appeared and was able to cut the tentacles incredibly fast—at the speed of light. Eventually, Black disappeared, and everything became uninfected again. Importantly: there is an unknown source of Black. That's established canon. 🖤 What happened to Black? Black disappeared. It isn't currently attacking the Duck Kingdom, and the world is no longer infected. Meanwhile, Duckey eventually ended up safe upstairs in a house in the Duck Kingdom. 🦆🏠
